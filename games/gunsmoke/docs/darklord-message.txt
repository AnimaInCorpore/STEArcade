Please play for a few minutes, try all three shooting directions, and tap
and release Esc to exit normally. Then please ZIP and return GUNTEST.TXT,
COUNTERS.BIN, RATE.BIN and BUILD.JSN, plus LATEPC.BIN and CRASH.TXT if present.
