Thanks again for testing. I've made CIRCLE.PRG to replace PULSE. It shows
a circle, grid and colour strips at the top and bottom. That should make
it easier to see whether a timing change shifts any of the colours.

Please run CIRCLE.PRG from CIRCLE.ZIP on the same setup. With both
borders open, look for both strips, steady colours and no flicker.
It runs at 16 MHz with cache on.

Tap 1 or 2 to move the top pulse earlier or later, and 3 or 4 for the
bottom pulse. 5 and 6 adjust the bottom pulse width, and 7 and 8 adjust
the top width. 0 restores the starting settings. The README has the
full key list.

If you get a clean full-height picture, press C to mark it. If it
scrambles, press X to record it and close the lower border. Check whether
the remaining picture clears. Press R to save and exit. Could you send the CIRCnn.TXT logs and a photo of any setting that
shows both strips cleanly?

Many thanks!
